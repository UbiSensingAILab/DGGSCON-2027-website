About the conference

DGGS organize the Earth into systems of cells, creating a common framework for connecting and analysing geospatial information.

A DGGS is a spatial reference system that uses a hierarchical tessellation of cells to partition and address the globe. When observations, model outputs and statistics share one set of cells, heterogeneous data can be integrated and analysed from local to global scales.

DGGS have progressed from research frameworks to international standards and open-source software, and attract growing interest across academia, industry and the GeoAI community. DGGSCon 2027 brings together researchers, students, software and standards developers, and practitioners across the public and private sectors.

Venue & travel

The conference is held at Banff Centre, in the mountains of Banff National Park, Alberta.

Banff Centre is a campus built for gatherings of this kind, with lecture theatres, breakout rooms and accommodation on site. The town of Banff is a short walk away, and Calgary International Airport is about 90 minutes by road.
